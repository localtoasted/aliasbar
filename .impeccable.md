## Design Context

### Users

AliasBar serves Mac users who reuse shell shortcuts and AI prompts throughout the day. They want to retrieve either kind of saved text without breaking focus, remember fewer names, and stay on the keyboard.

### Brand Personality

Direct, capable, and quietly playful. The product should feel fast and trustworthy without sounding technical for its own sake. It should treat aliases and prompts as equal parts of one personal shortcut library.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a refined macOS-native visual language with dark graphite surfaces, warm off-white type, and one controlled accent. Favor sharp typography, compact spacing, and a memorable symbol over glow, glass, terminal clichés, or generic AI gradients. Marketing images should show real product states and legible content.

### Design Principles

1. Show aliases and prompts with equal weight.
2. Lead with the product in use; keep explanations short.
3. Use one clear focal point per image or screen.
4. Make keyboard speed visible without turning the brand into a terminal app cliché.
5. Keep every asset crisp at Retina and README display sizes.
